# TapType Privacy Policy — Design Directions

## Three stylistic approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pit-Lane Paper** | An editorial racing notebook translated into a calm policy page: off-white paper, precise blue rules, and restrained coral signals. It makes required legal information feel operational and readable rather than ceremonial. | 0.07 |
| **Arcade Ledger** | A compact, high-contrast information sheet with pixel-grid references and dark ink typography. The tone is playful but disciplined, like a scorecard that happens to explain privacy. | 0.04 |
| **Clearwater System** | A quiet civic-service layout with pale blue modules and generous reading space. It frames trust as a useful product feature without borrowing from corporate security clichés. | 0.09 |

## Chosen approach: Pit-Lane Paper

### Design Movement

**Contemporary editorial wayfinding with a motorsport-paddock influence.** The page uses the visual clarity of race communications—numbers, rules, signals, and lanes—but trades spectacle for credibility. It is not a dashboard and not a dark gaming landing page; it is a legible public document with a controlled competitive edge.

### Core Principles

1. **Reading first:** Long-form policy content must remain easy to scan, follow, and quote on both mobile and desktop.
2. **Operational confidence:** The page should feel like a maintained part of the game, using well-spaced rules, labels, dates, and visible update paths.
3. **Purposeful asymmetry:** A narrow numbered rail anchors the document while the policy copy occupies an uninterrupted reading lane.
4. **Quiet character:** Decorative details are compact and functional—no gaming clichés, neon glow, or unearned visual noise.

### Color Philosophy

Warm ivory creates the visual cue of a well-kept printed policy, making long reading less sterile than pure white. **TapType blue (#1769FF)** owns interactive and navigational signals, while coral (#F05A47) is reserved for the racing accent, document-state marker, and small moments requiring attention. Ink (#152238) carries all essential text for dependable contrast.

### Layout Paradigm

The document is organized as a **continuous reading lane beside a fixed race-marker rail**, not a centered card grid. On wide screens, the rail supplies section numbers and anchors while the main article flows in an editorial column. On smaller screens, the rail collapses into a compact horizontal status strip so the text remains the priority.

### Signature Elements

1. **Race-marker rail:** A cobalt vertical rule with numbered checkpoints ties headings to navigation.
2. **Corner flags:** Small coral geometric flag corners appear beside key labels and the brand mark.
3. **Data-route diagram:** A light, abstract keyboard-to-shield illustration makes the otherwise text-only topic feel specific to TapType.

### Interaction Philosophy

Navigation behaves like a clear route map. Section links underline and snap to their reading checkpoint; buttons have a firm pressed state. There are no gimmicks, auto-playing animations, or distractions from the policy content.

### Animation

The only motion is a short 180ms hover/active transition on anchors, buttons, and the information tiles. The page respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; there are no continuous animations. The route indicators may fade into view on load using opacity and a subtle 4px upward transform only.

### Typography System

**Space Grotesk** is used for the fast, engineered display hierarchy and compact metadata. **Source Serif 4** is used for the policy text, giving legal paragraphs a relaxed reading texture while maintaining a contemporary feel. Headings remain short and firm; body copy uses a generous line-height and a narrow measure.

### Brand Essence

**TapType is a competitive typing-racing game for players who want every keystroke to count—built with transparent product practices, not opaque game mechanics.**

Personality: **precise, spirited, dependable**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are direct and plain-spoken. CTAs describe an action instead of offering generic encouragement. Microcopy should be specific about data, choices, and practical consequences.

> "Privacy, without the pit stops."

> "Jump to the data that matters to you."

### Wordmark & Logo

The logo is a compact **keyboard keycap accelerating into a forward chevron**, with a cursor-like notch cut through the middle. It is an icon-first mark; the TapType name sits beside it in a tracked, custom-feeling Space Grotesk wordmark rather than a default-font treatment.

### Signature Brand Color

**TapType Blue — #1769FF**

## Style Decisions

- Warm ivory remains the dominant field for every policy and support-resource page; dark navy is reserved for contained emphasis panels.
- Every policy page uses a continuous blue route-marker system with numbered checkpoints; coral is limited to flags, state markers, and corner accents.
- The TapType wordmark uses deliberately compact, engineered spacing alongside the keycap-chevron mark rather than ordinary navigation typography.
